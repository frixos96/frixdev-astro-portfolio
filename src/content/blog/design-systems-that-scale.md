A design system is only as good as the constraints it enforces. The best ones
make the right thing the easy thing.

## Principles I keep coming back to

1. **Tokens before components.** Colors, spacing, and typography first.
2. **Compose, don't configure.** Small pieces beat giant prop APIs.
3. **Document by example.** A live demo beats a paragraph of prose.

Keep it small, keep it consistent, and let it grow with real needs — not
hypothetical ones.
