Most side projects die not from lack of skill, but from lack of scope. Here's
what keeps mine alive.

## Ship the smallest useful thing

If you can't describe it in one sentence, it's too big. Cut until you can.

## Make it public early

Nothing motivates like a deadline you announced to other people. Push to
production on day one, even if it only shows "Hello world".

## Automate the boring parts

CI, deploys, and formatting should never cost you a decision. Set them up once
and forget they exist.
